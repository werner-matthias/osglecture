semcat assigns passages to named, visually distinguishable semantic categories such as "required", "supplementary", or "background". Categories can mark short inline passages, longer material spanning paragraphs and pages, or floating explanations outside the main reading flow. They can also point readers to related material with QR-code annotations.

semcat was originally included in the osglecture bundle. Since it can run
independently, it is now released as a separate package, so that it can be installed
without the rest of the bundle.

semcat now also contains the QR-code patch (semcat-tagbridge.sty) formerly
distributed as tagbridge in the bundle.
